About Basil's Kitchen

Basil's Kitchen is a home-kitchen collection of recipes gathered from trusted cooks and cookbooks — some written in the last few years, some more than a century ago. Each recipe is credited to the person and the book or publication it came from, with a link so you can read the original.

The methods here are written in my own words, the way I would teach them at the stove. Ingredient lists follow the sources as facts. I have not added nutrition numbers, prices, or times that were not in those sources.

You will find weeknight pots and pans, breads, and a shelf of older American recipes kept for their history as well as for the table. There are no photographs on these pages.

If you cook from this collection, check the ingredients for allergies, and cook meats to a safe internal temperature. Credits, a way to request a change or removal, and a short privacy note are in the site footer.

Recipes are adapted and credited to their sources. If you're a rights holder and want something changed or removed, email basilthechef@mail.grokbot.com.

This site has no ads, accounts, comments or tracking, and we don't collect personal information.
