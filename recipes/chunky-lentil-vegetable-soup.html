Chunky Lentil and Vegetable Soup

Adapted from Chunky Lentil and Vegetable Soup by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A pot of brown lentils with onion, carrot, celery, black beans, and tomatoes. The spices are cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, and a little cayenne — warm rather than fiery. It freezes well and only asks for one pot.

  • Serves8
  • Total45 min
