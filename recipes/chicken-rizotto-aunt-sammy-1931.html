Chicken Rizotto (Aunt Sammy, 1931)

Adapted from Chicken Rizotto by Ruth Van Deman and Fanny Walker Yeatman, Aunt Sammy's Radio Recipes, Revised (USDA Bureau of Home Economics, 1931).

A 1931 USDA leftover dish: broth made from chicken bones, onion cooked in fat, rice simmered in the broth without much stirring, then chopped cooked chicken and a generous shower of grated cheese. The book spells it "rizotto." This is a U.S. government publication; the method below is written in my own words from the book's facts.

  • Totalabout 25 min rice simmer, plus making broth from the bones
  • CuisineAmerican (Italian-inspired)
