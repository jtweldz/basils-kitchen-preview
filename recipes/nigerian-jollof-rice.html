Nigerian Jollof Rice

Adapted from Nigerian Jollof Rice by Serious Eats, Serious Eats.

Jollof rice starts with a blended stew of tomatoes, red pepper, onion, and a little hot chili, which is reduced twice so the rice cooks in a deep red sauce. Bay, curry powder, and thyme season the pot. Converted long-grain rice or Golden Sella basmati holds its shape. Nigerian stock is called for; use a good homemade or prepared version.

  • Serves6
  • Total1 hr 30 min
  • CuisineWest African
