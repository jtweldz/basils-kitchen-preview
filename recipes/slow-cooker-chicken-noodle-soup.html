Slow Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup

Adapted from Slow Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Bone-in chicken breasts cook on a bed of onion, carrot, and celery in plenty of water, then you shred the meat and simmer egg noodles in the same broth. The noodles go in at the end so they don't turn soft.

  • Serves8
  • Total270 min
  • CuisineAmerican
