Slow Cooker Chicken and Dumplings

Adapted from Slow Cooker Chicken & Dumplings by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Chicken, vegetables, and herbs cook in the slow cooker until the meat shreds easily, then spoonfuls of a simple dumpling batter go in for the last stretch. The broth is water, so salt at the end matters.

  • Serves6
  • Total290 min
