Toasted Orzo Chicken Soup

Adapted from Toasted Orzo Chicken Soup by Rebecca Firkser, Bon Appétit.

Chicken thighs simmer in a lightly spiced broth, then toasted orzo is cooked in that same pot. Fennel seed, cardamom, and clove are bloomed in the oil at the start; lemon zest goes on the orzo, and lemon juice and greens go in at the end. The method also uses 3 cups of water with the 4 cups of broth.

  • Serves4–6
  • Total50 minutes
  • CuisineAmerican
