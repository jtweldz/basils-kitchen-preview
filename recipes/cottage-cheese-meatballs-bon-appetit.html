Cottage Cheese Meatballs

Adapted from Cottage Cheese Meatballs by Jesse Szewczyk, Bon Appétit.

Cottage cheese mixed into ground beef makes a lighter, more tender meatball and stretches a pound of meat. The balls are browned in a skillet, then finished in the oven. They keep in the refrigerator or the freezer.

  • Serves4
  • Total1 hour
  • CuisineAmerican
