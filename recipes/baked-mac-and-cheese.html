Baked Mac and Cheese

Adapted from Baked Mac and Cheese by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

This is a baked macaroni with a stovetop cheese sauce and a seasoned crumb topping. The sauce starts with broth and cream rather than a flour roux, then the pasta goes into a dish in two layers so the crumbs sit on a generous cheese blanket.

The ingredient list includes chicken broth. Use vegetable broth if you want the dish to be fully meatless.

  • Serves6
  • Total55 min
  • CuisineAmerican
