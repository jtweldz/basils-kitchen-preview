Vegetarian Shepherd's Pie

Adapted from Vegetarian Shepherd's Pie by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A vegetable filling of mushrooms, carrots, celery, and peas under a lid of mashed potatoes. Cooked lentils are optional if you want more substance. You will need about 4 cups of mashed potatoes already made.

  • Serves6
  • Total55 min
