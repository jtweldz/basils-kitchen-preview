Slow Cooker Spaghetti Sauce

Adapted from Slow Cooker Spaghetti Sauce by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A simple marinara that simmers unattended: crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, onion, garlic, basil, oregano, a little brown sugar, balsamic vinegar, and butter. It makes enough to sauce pasta now and freeze a container for later.

  • Serves7
  • Total250 min
  • CuisineItalian
