Pigs in Blankets (Betty Crocker, 1943)

Adapted from Pigs in Blankets, Your Share by the Betty Crocker Home Service Department, General Mills (1943).

A wartime supper from General Mills' 1943 booklet Your Share: wieners simmered, then wrapped in thin squares of biscuit dough and baked hot and fast, with the ends of the sausages left showing. The booklet assumes you already have a biscuit dough; it does not give a separate dough recipe.

  • Serves8-10 (1 lb wieners)
  • Totalabout 30 min
  • CuisineAmerican
