Tamale Pie (Goudiss, 1918)

From Tamale Pie by C. Houston Goudiss and Alberta M. Goudiss, Foods That Will Win the War and How to Cook Them (1918, Project Gutenberg #15464). Public domain (U.S., published 1918).

A First World War "meat-extension" pie: a seasoned meat-and-tomato filling between layers of cornmeal mush. The 1918 book is in the public domain. The ingredient list in the book does not include cayenne, but the method calls for ⅛ teaspoon; I have left that as the authors wrote it. The book gives no oven temperature.

  • Totalabout 30 min mush + 30 min bake (book times)
  • CuisineAmerican

Ingredients

From the 1918 book

To the cornmeal and 1 teaspoon salt, add boiling water. Cook one-half hour. Brown onion in fat, add meat. Add salt, 1/8 teaspoon cayenne, the tomatoes and green peppers. Grease baking dish, put in layer of cornmeal mush, add seasoned meat, and cover with mush. Bake one-half hour.
