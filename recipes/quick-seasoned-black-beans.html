Quick Seasoned Black Beans

Adapted from Quick Seasoned Black Beans by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Canned black beans warmed with jalapeño, garlic, and cumin. Leave the liquid in the cans — it becomes the sauce. Fifteen minutes, and useful next to rice, eggs, or roasted vegetables.

  • Serves6
  • Total15 min
  • CuisineSouthwest
