Chicken Adobo

Adapted from Filipino-Style Chicken Adobo by the Serious Eats test kitchen, Serious Eats.

Bone-in chicken legs braised in soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic, bay, and peppercorns. The chicken is browned first, then simmered in the sauce until tender. It is even better after a night in the refrigerator. Serve with steamed rice or garlic fried rice, and broil the skin briefly if you want it crisp.

  • Serves4
  • Total50 min
  • CuisineFilipino
