Sheet Pan Chicken Sausage Dinner

Adapted from Sheet Pan Chicken Sausage Dinner by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A weeknight tray of already-cooked chicken sausage with sweet potato, broccoli, pepper, and red onion. Everything is tossed in oil and dried oregano, then roasted until the vegetables brown. The sausage only needs to heat through and take on some color.

  • Serves4
  • Total45 min
  • CuisineAmerican
