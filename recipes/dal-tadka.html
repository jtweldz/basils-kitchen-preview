Dal Tadka

Adapted from Dal Tadka by Swasthi Shreekanth, Swasthi's Recipes.

Soft-cooked toor dal finished with a pan of spiced onion and tomato, then a last pour of hot ghee tadka — cumin, garlic, dried chilies, and a pinch of asafoetida. A squeeze of lemon at the table brightens it. Serve with rice or flatbread.

  • Serves4
  • Total40 min
  • CuisineIndian
