Chicken Tagine

Adapted from Chicken Tagine by Serious Eats, Serious Eats.

Chicken leg quarters, marinated with lemon and spices, then simmered over a great quantity of onions until the sauce cooks down to a jammy relish with preserved lemon and olives. In Morocco that reduced onion-and-oil sauce is called deghmira. You can use a Dutch oven; a traditional tagine isn't required. Marinate the chicken at least 2 hours, or overnight.

  • Serves4
  • Total4 hr 40 min to 10 hr 40 min (includes marinating)
  • CuisineMoroccan
