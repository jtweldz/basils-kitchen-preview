Slow Cooker Pulled Pork

Adapted from Slow Cooker Pulled Pork by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Pork butt, cut into chunks and rubbed with paprika, brown sugar, and garlic, cooks over onion and garlic until it shreds. A few minutes under the broiler is optional if you like crisp edges. Good in sandwiches, tacos, or bowls, and it freezes well.

  • Serves8
  • Total255 min
  • CuisineAmerican
