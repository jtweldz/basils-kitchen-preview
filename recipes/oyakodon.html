Oyakodon

Adapted from Oyakodon by Namiko Hirasawa Chen, Just One Cookbook.

Oyakodon means "parent and child bowl": chicken and egg simmered in a sweet-savory dashi sauce and served over rice. Have the rice cooked before you start the pan, and don't try to cook more than two servings in one medium skillet — the sauce should just cover the onions.

  • Serves2
  • Total25 min
  • CuisineJapanese
