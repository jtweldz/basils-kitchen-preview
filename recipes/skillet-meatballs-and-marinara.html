Skillet Meatballs and Marinara

Adapted from Skillet Meatballs and Marinara by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Meatballs made from Italian sausage, browned in a skillet, then simmered in a quick tomato sauce in the same pan. Pasta cooks separately. The onion is split: half goes into the meatballs, half into the sauce.

  • Serves6
  • Total45 min
