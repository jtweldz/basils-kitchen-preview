Gyeranjjim (Steamed Eggs)

Adapted from Gyeranjjim by Maangchi, maangchi.com.

A soft, savory Korean steamed egg custard, seasoned with salted shrimp (saeujeot) or fish sauce and finished with green onion. You can steam the bowl on the stove or cook it in the microwave. It is usually served with rice.
