Misir Wat (Red Lentils)

Adapted from Misir Wat by Kimberly Killebrew, The Daring Gourmet.

Ethiopian red lentils cooked with onion, tomato, niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter), and berbere. The berbere and niter kibbeh go in twice — once with the aromatics, once at the end — so the spice stays bright. The source strongly recommends homemade berbere. Serve with injera. The broth in the ingredient list is chicken broth; use a vegetable broth if you want a meatless pot.

  • Serves6
  • Total1 hr 5 min
  • CuisineEthiopian
