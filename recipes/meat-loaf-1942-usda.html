Meat Loaf (1942 USDA)

Adapted from Meat Loaf, Meat for Thrifty Meals by the USDA Bureau of Home Economics (1942).

A wartime loaf from the USDA's 1942 booklet Meat for Thrifty Meals: ground lean meat stretched with a white sauce, breadcrumbs, and vegetables cooked in salt-pork or suet drippings. The book says to bake it "well done to the center." This is a U.S. government publication; the method is written in my own words from the book's facts.
