Turkey Chili

Adapted from Turkey Chili by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A one-pot chili with ground turkey, three kinds of beans, corn, fire-roasted tomatoes, and a poblano pepper. The tomato paste and a short simmer pull it together without a long afternoon on the stove.
