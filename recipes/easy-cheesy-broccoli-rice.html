Easy Cheesy Broccoli Rice

Adapted from Easy Cheesy Broccoli Rice by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Rice cooked in the same pot you finish the dish in, with chopped broccoli and two cheeses folded through at the end. A pinch of cayenne is optional if you want a little heat.
