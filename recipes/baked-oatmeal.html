Baked Oatmeal

Adapted from Baked Oatmeal by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

A pan of oats baked with yogurt, milk, and fruit until the center is set. Ginger does more of the seasoning work here than cinnamon. It is a good make-ahead breakfast; leftover squares reheat well.

  • Serves6
  • Total60 min
  • CuisineAmerican
