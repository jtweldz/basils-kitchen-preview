Beef Loaf (Aunt Sammy, 1931)

Adapted from Beef Loaf by Ruth Van Deman and Fanny Walker Yeatman, Aunt Sammy's Radio Recipes, Revised (USDA Bureau of Home Economics, 1931).

A 1931 USDA meat loaf that starts by frying salt pork crisp, then uses some of the drippings for a white sauce and the rest to cook celery, parsley, and onion with breadcrumbs. Everything is mixed by hand, shaped on a rack, and baked uncovered, without water in the pan. It can be served hot or sliced cold. This is a U.S. government publication; the method is written in my own words from the book's facts.

  • Totalbake 1 1/4 hr, plus prep
