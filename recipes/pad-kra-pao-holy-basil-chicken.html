Pad Kra Pao (Holy Basil Chicken)

Adapted from Pad Kra Pao (Holy Basil Stir-Fry) by Pailin Chongchitnant, Hot Thai Kitchen.

A hot, fast Thai stir-fry of coarsely ground chicken with garlic, chilies, and holy basil, served with jasmine rice and a fried egg. Holy basil (kraphao) is the herb that gives the dish its name; if you cannot find it, read the source page for Pailin's guidance on substitutes. Adjust the Thai chilies to your heat tolerance.
