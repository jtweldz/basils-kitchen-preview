Sheet Pan BBQ Meatballs

Adapted from Sheet Pan BBQ Meatballs by Beth Moncel, Budget Bytes.

Turkey meatballs glazed with barbecue sauce, roasted on a sheet pan beside peppers, onion, and pineapple, with a pot of rice going on the stove. Two pans keep the meatballs from steaming in the vegetable juices.
