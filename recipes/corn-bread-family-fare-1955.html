Corn Bread (Family Fare, 1955)

Adapted from Corn Bread, Family Fare: Food Management and Recipes (USDA, 1955).

A plain USDA corn bread from the 1955 booklet Family Fare: cornmeal, a little baking soda and baking powder, eggs, "sour milk," and melted fat, baked in an 8-inch pan. In 1955, "sour milk" meant naturally soured milk, not milk that has spoiled in the refrigerator. Today, use buttermilk, or stir 1 tablespoon of vinegar into each cup of milk. This is a U.S. government publication; the method is written in my own words from the book's facts.
