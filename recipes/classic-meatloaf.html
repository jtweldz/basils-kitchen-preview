Classic Meatloaf

Adapted from My Dad's Favorite Meatloaf by Elise Bauer, Simply Recipes.

A loaf of ground beef and sausage bound with breadcrumbs and eggs, with a cooked mixture of onion, celery, carrot, and green onion mixed through, and ketchup split between the meat and the top. It takes a little time, but most of that is in the oven.

  • Serves6
  • Total100 min
  • CuisineAmerican
