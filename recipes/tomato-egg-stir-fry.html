Tomato Egg Stir-Fry

Adapted from Stir-Fried Tomato and Egg by The Woks of Life (Bill, Judy, Sarah, and Kaitlin Leung), The Woks of Life.

A home-style Chinese stir-fry of soft scrambled eggs and tomatoes in a light sweet-savory sauce. The eggs are cooked first and set aside so they stay tender, then the tomatoes are cooked down and the two are combined. It takes about 10 minutes and is usually eaten with rice.

  • Serves2
  • Total10 min
  • CuisineChinese
