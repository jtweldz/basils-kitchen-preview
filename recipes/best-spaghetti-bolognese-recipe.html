Spaghetti Bolognese

Adapted from The best spaghetti bolognese recipe by Andrew Balmer, BBC Good Food.

A long-simmered meat sauce with bacon, beef, soffritto, red wine, and tomatoes, finished with Parmesan. It is worth the time: the sauce thickens and sweetens as it cooks, and it freezes well. A fresh chili is optional.

  • Serves6
  • Total2 hr 15 min
  • CuisineItalian
