Weeknight Pumpkin Chili

Adapted from Weeknight Pumpkin Chili by Kendra Vaculin, Bon Appétit.

A short-simmer chili that uses canned pumpkin with the beef, black beans, and pinto beans. The pumpkin is cooked with smoked paprika and cumin until some of its moisture cooks off, which gives the pot body without a long reduction. Worcestershire sauce goes in at two points.

  • Serves4
  • Total35 minutes
  • CuisineAmerican
