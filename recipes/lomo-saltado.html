Lomo Saltado

Adapted from Lomo Saltado by Serious Eats, Serious Eats.

A Peruvian stir-fry of quick-cooking steak with red onion, tomato, scallion, and ají amarillo, seasoned with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and ginger. It is served with both French fries and rice — a mix of Chinese wok cooking and Peruvian flavors. Get the pan very hot and cook in batches so the meat and vegetables sear instead of steaming.

  • Serves2
  • Total25 min
  • CuisinePeruvian
